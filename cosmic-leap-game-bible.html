🎯 Elevator Pitch

Cosmic Leap is a 3D gravity-platformer where players leap between asteroid worlds with ever-changing gravity rules. Each of the 7 galaxy worlds introduces a new physics mechanic — from crystal low-gravity to ice frictionless surfaces. The final world combines all mechanics at once. Players earn Star Shards by winning, defeating others, and completing levels — and can spend them to continue, unlock skins, and revive. Solo, co-op, race, and arena modes keep gameplay fresh.

Level Design Document
The 7 Aether Galaxies
🧭 Design Philosophy

Each galaxy introduces one core gravity mechanic that builds on the last. Difficulty scales by forcing players to combine mechanics from previous worlds. World 7 — The Core — reuses all prior mechanics simultaneously. Solo and co-op modes share the same geometry but unlock branching paths that require two players to access.

Character Roster
Playable Characters & Ability Trees
🧑‍🚀 Character Philosophy

Each character has a base move set (jump, dash, grab) plus 4 unique abilities that unlock through the ability tree. Abilities cost Star Shards to unlock permanently. Characters are not pay-to-win — abilities change playstyle, not raw power. All abilities can be earned through gameplay without spending real money.

Monetisation Design
Star Shard Economy
💡 Economy Principle

Players start with 500 free Star Shards — enough to play for several sessions and experience the full game loop before spending anything. The economy rewards skill: good players can sustain indefinitely through shard earnings. Spending accelerates progress, not gates content. No pay-to-win: all purchases are cosmetic or convenience.

Technical Architecture
Full Stack Breakdown
🏗️ Architecture Principle

Server-authoritative throughout. The client renders only — all game state, token transactions, match results, and anti-cheat run server-side. No client value is trusted without server confirmation. This prevents token cheating, speedhacking, and result manipulation.
